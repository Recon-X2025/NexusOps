## What this corrects/sharpens

run-172721 §3 reported that the authorised Finance approver can't reach the payroll approval, and called it "an RBAC-configuration inconsistency." This note pins the **root cause**: the payroll **approval action** and the payroll **surface that hosts it** are gated on **different permission modules**, and no non-owner role holds both.

## The evidence (verified this pass)

Every procedure and gate on the payroll surface uses the **`payroll`** module — *except the approve action*, which is the lone exception:

| Element | Gate | Module |
|---|---|---|
| `/app/payroll` route-guard | `resolveRouteModule` → | **`payroll`** (`apps/web/src/lib/route-permissions.ts:73`) |
| `payroll.runs.list` (loads the run list) | `permissionProcedure("payroll","read")` | **`payroll`** (`apps/api/src/routers/payroll.ts:248`) |
| `payroll.runs.get` (loads a run + its cycle) | `permissionProcedure("payroll","read")` | **`payroll`** (`payroll.ts:258`) |
| every other payroll mutation (lock, compute, computePayslips, generateStatutory, …) | `permissionProcedure("payroll","write")` | **`payroll`** |
| **`payroll.runs.approve`** (HR/Finance/CFO) | bare `protectedProcedure` + hand-rolled check | **`hr`** for HR step, **`financial`** for Finance/CFO (`payroll.ts:575,588`) |

Role holdings (from `packages/types/src/rbac-matrix.ts`):
- `payroll.read`: **hr_manager, hr_analyst** (+ owner)
- `financial.write`: **finance_manager, procurement_admin** (+ owner)
- **Intersection among non-owner roles: empty.**

## Root cause

`payroll.runs.approve` was deliberately authorised against the **`financial`** module for the Finance/CFO steps (correct in spirit — the Finance Controller and CFO are financial-domain roles). But the **page** that renders the approve control, and the **read procedures** that load the run onto it, are authorised against the **`payroll`** module. The two gates were never reconciled, and no non-owner role bridges them. Result:

- **finance_manager** (has `financial.write`) → **blocked at the page** ("Access Restricted — payroll") and would 403 on `payroll.runs.list/get` anyway → cannot see or open the run to approve.
- **hr_manager** (has `payroll.read`) → can open the page, but **403 on the Finance step** ("financial.write required").
- **owner** → has both, but SoD forbids doing more than one of HR/Finance/CFO.

So the chain is **not completable through the product** by role-appropriate accounts. This is the wiring mismatch, not SoD (SoD is a separate, correct check at `payroll.ts:604–616`).

## Classification

- **Not "working as designed":** the role the action is built for cannot reach the action.
- **Fairly readable two ways, same fix:** (a) *code* — `approve` is the only payroll procedure not gated on the `payroll` module, and that inconsistency is the defect; (b) *config* — the seed roles are under-provisioned (a real org would also grant its Finance Controller/CFO `payroll.read`).

## Fix options (either resolves it)

1. **Reconcile the surface gate with the action gate:** let the `/app/payroll` route-guard and `payroll.runs.list/get` admit a `financial.write` approver too (an OR: `payroll.read` **or** `financial.write`), so the person authorised to approve can load and see the run.
2. **Provision the roles:** grant `payroll.read` to `finance_manager` and to whatever role plays CFO.

With either, the chain runs with distinct identities: **HR = hr@ (`hr.write` + `payroll.read`)**, **Finance = finance@ (`financial.write`)**, **CFO = a second distinct `financial.write` holder** — and step 13 (statutory outputs) becomes reachable.

## Note on the approval surface

The payroll approvals are executed **from the payroll run itself** (the per-step Execute cards), **not** the cross-module `/app/approvals` queue. Checking that queue (run-172721 §2) confirmed there is **no alternate surface** for the finance approver — which is why the page lockout fully blocks them.
