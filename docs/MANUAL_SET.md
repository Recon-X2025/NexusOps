# First Payroll Cycle — What You Handle Manually

This guide lists the things the platform does **not** do automatically in your first payroll
cycle, so nothing is a surprise. Everything to do with money in this first cycle is either
computed correctly by the system **or** listed here as something you handle yourself. If it is
on this list, treat it as manual for now: do the step outside the system and keep your own
record. Each item below says exactly what the system will and will not show, and what you
should do instead.

Most of these are first-cycle limitations that will be automated in later releases.

---

## 1. Professional tax — Kerala and Tamil Nadu

Professional tax (PT) is deducted automatically for most states, including Karnataka.

**Kerala and Tamil Nadu are the exception in this first cycle.** These two states collect
professional tax on a half-yearly basis, and the system does **not** compute or deduct it yet
for employees working in them.

**What to do:** calculate the half-yearly professional tax for your Kerala and Tamil Nadu
employees using the state's own slab, deduct it manually, and remit it to the state on the
usual schedule. The payslip for these employees will show professional tax as zero — that is
expected, not an error. Keep your own record of what you deducted and paid.

Other states that collect PT on a quarterly or half-yearly cycle (for example Sikkim or
Puducherry) are handled the same way — manually — if you employ anyone there. Monthly-PT
states are automatic.

---

## 2. Input tax credit on purchases (GST)

The system can match your purchase records against your supplier GST statement and flag
credits that are blocked by law. It does **not** yet handle every part of an input-tax-credit
claim — reverse-charge purchases, the time limit for claiming a credit, and the automatic
posting of credits into your books are not wired for the first cycle.

**What to do:** have your chartered accountant claim input tax credit manually for the first
cycle, as they do today. Use the system's reconciliation output as a helpful reference, not as
the finished claim.

---

## 3. Medical and Conveyance allowances are not paid

The salary structure no longer shows Medical and Conveyance allowance fields. These were
allowances that the system does not pay — any amount entered against them would have been
ignored and paid as zero.

**What to do:** do not try to pay employees through a Medical or Conveyance allowance. If you
need to give an equivalent amount, fold it into the regular salary components. Nothing is lost
by their removal — the total salary is unchanged.

---

## 4. Full and final settlement — records to keep

The system now composes a leaving employee's full and final settlement inside the product:
last salary for days worked, leave encashment, gratuity, minus any recoveries, giving the net
amount payable. This figure and each of its parts are saved.

Two things remain your responsibility:

- **The clock.** The settlement is due within **two working days of the employee's last
  working day**. The system starts that clock from the last working day you record, but paying
  on time is a manual step — make the payment and mark it done.
- **Evidence.** Keep your own signed record of the settlement: the final calculation you paid,
  proof of any recoveries (notice-period shortfall, advances, company assets not returned), and
  the employee's acknowledgement. The system stores the computed figures for reference, but the
  signed settlement document and its supporting proof are yours to retain.
- **It records only once — check before you confirm.** A settlement can be recorded **once per
  employee** and cannot be edited or redone in the system afterwards. Review each figure on the
  preview before confirming; if something later turns out wrong, correct it in your own records
  and outside the system.

---

## 5. Uploaded documents are not saved in this environment

Several places in the product let you attach or upload a file. On the environment you are using
for the first cycle, **most of these uploads are not stored** — the file is not saved anywhere
and cannot be retrieved later. The affected uploads are:

- General document uploads (the document library)
- Form 16
- Profile photos / avatars
- Purchase-order attachments in procurement
- Electronic-signature key files
- The three offboarding attachments: **separation** documents, **clearance** documents, and
  **security-clearance** documents

**The one exception is the payslip.** Payslip PDFs are generated fresh each time and download
correctly — you can hand employees their payslips in the first cycle without any file storage.

**What to do:** keep these documents in your own secure storage (your existing HR or finance
drive) for now. In particular, because employees are confirmed from day one, remember that
**separation and clearance forms for anyone who leaves must be kept by you**, not in the
system. Form 16 is issued only after the financial year ends, so it is not a first-cycle
concern.

---

## 6. Identity documents are sighted, not stored

By design, the system does **not** store images of Aadhaar or PAN cards. The intended process
is that HR sights the original document in person and records that it was verified.

**What to do:** follow your normal in-person verification. Record that you have seen the
document; do not expect to upload or retrieve a copy from the system.

---

## 7. Leave types and labels

The system shows two leave balances: **Annual Leave** and **Sick / Casual Leave**.

- Casual leave is **not** a separate balance of its own — it is grouped under
  "Sick / Casual Leave."
- The rule that treats a short absence (three days or fewer) differently is **not** enforced
  automatically.

**What to do:** if your policy distinguishes casual leave from sick leave, or applies a
short-absence rule, track that distinction yourself and apply it when approving leave. The
balances shown are still accurate totals; only the finer classification is manual.

---

## 8. Other pay items handled outside the system this cycle

- **Leave Travel Allowance (LTA):** if LTA is part of a salary, it is currently taxed in full.
  The tax exemption for actual travel is **not** applied by the system this cycle. Employees who
  are eligible can claim the exemption when they file their own income-tax return; if you want
  to give effect to it in payroll, do so manually and keep the proof of travel.
- **Bonus:** statutory or target bonuses are **not** processed through the salary structure yet.
  Pay any bonus outside the regular salary run and account for its tax separately for now.

---

## 9. Company (ROC) filings

Certain company-secretarial filings — for example director KYC and the annual company return —
do **not** have an automated filing path yet. **Handle these manually or through your company
secretary for the first cycle.** They are not part of the monthly payroll run.

---

## In short

The first cycle can run payroll, deduct statutory amounts for most states, produce correct
payslips, and settle a leaver — all inside the system. The manual set above is the short,
known list of exceptions. Do each of them the usual way, keep your own records, and later
releases will bring them into the product.
